# AeroGuard AI

## Offline Disaster Intelligence Powered by Google DeepMind's Gemma 4

AeroGuard AI is an AI-powered disaster response assistant designed to help communities, emergency responders, and disaster management teams make faster decisions during floods and climate-related emergencies.

The system combines **Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 multimodal AI**, computer vision, and drone intelligence workflows to analyze disaster situations and generate actionable emergency recommendations.

---

# Problem

Many communities experience delayed emergency response because of:

- Limited access to real-time information
- Poor connectivity during disasters
- Lack of intelligent decision-support systems
- Difficulty assessing affected areas quickly

During emergencies, responders need fast answers:

- Where is the affected area?
- How severe is the situation?
- Who is at risk?
- What resources are required?
- What actions should be prioritized?

---

# Solution

AeroGuard AI provides an intelligent disaster analysis system powered by Gemma 4.

The system analyzes emergency information and generates structured response reports to support faster decision-making.

AeroGuard AI helps users:

- Analyze disaster descriptions
- Analyze drone and disaster images
- Identify potential hazards
- Estimate affected areas
- Generate emergency response recommendations
- Support disaster management decisions

---

# How Gemma 4 Powers AeroGuard AI

Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 serves as the core reasoning engine behind AeroGuard AI.

Gemma 4 provides multimodal intelligence, allowing the system to understand both text and images.

- Situation information

## Gemma 4 Processing

Gemma 4 analyzes the information and generates:
